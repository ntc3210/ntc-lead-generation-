# Worked example: a channel plan for a voice-AI API company

> A teaching example built from `profile.md` in this folder (public
> information only; not affiliated with Deepgram). It shows **how to
> reason**, not what any real company does.

## Start with these

| # | Channel | Why it fits | Weekly effort | Cost | Legal risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Job posts** | A company hiring a "voice AI engineer" or posting about LiveKit or Pipecat is building voice right now. It's the clearest timing signal. | 2 hrs | Free | Low |
| 2 | **Communities** (LiveKit, Pipecat, voice-AI forums) | Developers, the champions, ask about accuracy and latency there in public. Answer helpfully; don't pitch. | 3 hrs | Free | Low if you follow the rules |
| 3 | **Cold email to technical leaders** at companies with a signal | Short, specific notes referencing the signal. The user approves every one. | 2 hrs | A separate sending domain, about $10–20/yr, plus a mailbox | High (CAN-SPAM, plus GDPR and PECR in Europe; no cold email to Germany) |

## Not now, and why
- **Cold calling:** developers and CTOs rarely take cold calls. Keep it for
  contact-centre operations leaders later, with humans dialling and
  numbers checked against Do-Not-Call lists. **Never use AI-voice cold
  calls**, which ironically would be this industry's own product (FCC
  ruling, Feb 2024).
- **Reddit:** watch for questions through search, and answer only where a
  subreddit allows it. It's a listening post, not a lead list.
- **Events:** high value for enterprise deals but costly. Revisit once
  the email channel shows which segment replies.

## Signals to search for
1. Job posts naming voice tools (LiveKit, Pipecat, Twilio Media Streams,
   "STT latency").
2. Launches of AI receptionists, voice agents or AI scribes (news, last 30
   days).
3. Seed to Series B funding for voice-AI, healthcare-scribe or collections
   startups.

## A first email (illustrative, before the user's edits)
```
Subject: your voice agent role

Hi <name>,

Saw <company> is hiring a <exact job title> to work on <thing quoted
from the post>. Teams building that usually hit <one specific problem,
e.g. latency on live calls> early.

<one line of real, public proof the user supplied>

Worth a 15-minute look? If not, just say so and I won't follow up.

<sender name>, <company>
<postal address> · Reply "no thanks" to stop hearing from me.
```
Jimmy checks it: the job post is linked, the proof is one the user gave,
there's no invented familiarity, and the postal address and opt-out are
present.

## What "working" looks like after 4 weeks
- At least 10% reply rate on signal-based emails. That's a rough rule of
  thumb, so set your own target from your first two weeks.
- Two community answers that someone thanked or followed up on.
- A lessons entry on which signal produced the replies.
