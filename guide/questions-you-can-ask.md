# Not sure what to ask? Try one of these.

Show a few of these, picked to suit where the user is, whenever they say
"I don't know what to ask", "what can you do?" or go quiet. Never show
the whole list at once.

## Getting started
- "Here's my company website: what do you think we sell, and to whom?"
- "Who should we be selling to that we aren't?"
- "Look at our customer stories and tell me what our best customers have
  in common."

## Finding leads
- "Find 10 companies that look like our best customer."
- "Which companies just raised money in our industry?"
- "Who is hiring for a job that means they need what we sell?"
- "Find people complaining online about the problem we solve."
- "Which companies are using a competitor we could replace?"
- "Where do our buyers hang out online?"

## Reaching them
- "Which two channels should I start with, and why?"
- "Write a first email to this lead. Short and honest."
- "Write a phone script for my team, with answers to the common
  objections."
- "What should I say on Reddit or in a forum without being spammy?"
- "Turn this lead list into a 3-step follow-up plan."

## Checking and improving
- "Is this email legal to send to someone in the UK, Canada or Germany?"
- "Why aren't people replying?" (paste the message)
- "Which of my leads should I contact first this week?"
- "What did we learn from last week?"

## Setup (step by step, with the cost first)
- "Help me set up a separate domain for outreach email."
- "How do I check phone numbers against the Do-Not-Call list?"
- "Can you search for leads automatically? What would it cost?"
