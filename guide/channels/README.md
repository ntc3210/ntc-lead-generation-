# Channel playbooks: where to find leads and how to reach them

> **For the assistant:** after the interview (`../interview.md`), pick
> **two or three** channels that fit this user's business, not all of
> them, and write the plan in `templates/channel-plan.md`. Read a
> channel's playbook in full before using it. Quote it in plain words.
>
> **Never-break rules for every channel:**
> 1. **Humans send and dial.** You draft; the user approves each message
>    before it's sent. You never place a call.
> 2. **No scraping** of LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, Google Maps and
>    Business Profiles, Yelp, Reddit, or any site whose terms forbid it.
>    LinkedIn is manual only. Reddit only through web-search titles and
>    snippets.
> 3. **Business contact details only.** No home addresses, personal
>    phones or personal social profiles. A guessed email is labelled as a
>    guess, with how to verify it.
> 4. **Evidence on every lead:** the URL or exact quote that shows why.
>    Never invent a quote, a person or a company.
> 5. **Check `my-company/do-not-contact.md`** before every message or
>    call. Anyone who says no goes on it, permanently.
> 6. **Communities:** be a member first, give before asking, read the
>    rules before mentioning the product.
>
> *Practical guidance, not legal advice. Sources in each playbook were
> checked on 11 October 2026; anything marked "unverified" wasn't.*

## The channels at a glance

**Cost** is the money the user spends, beyond their own time and their
Claude plan. **Legal risk** is how easy it is to break a law or platform
rule if the playbook isn't followed (low / medium / high). **Speed** is
how soon it usually produces conversations.

| Channel | Best for | Cost | Legal risk | Speed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Company websites](websites.md) | Researching fit and finding the right person at B2B targets | Free | Low | Days (research only; pair with an outreach channel) |
| [Cold email](cold-email.md) | Short, personal, evidence-based notes to B2B buyers | Low: a sending domain (about $10–20 a year) plus a mailbox (about $7 or more per user a month for a business mailbox) | **High**: varies by country; Germany and Canada strict | 1–3 weeks (after 2–3 weeks of warm-up) |
| [Cold calling](cold-calling.md) | Buyers who answer the phone at work: owners, operators, managers | Low: a phone; US DNC access free for 5 area codes, $85 each after (FY2027, from 1 Oct 2026) | **High**: DNC registers; AI or recorded voices need consent | Days |
| [Automated outreach](automated-outreach.md) | Reliable follow-up once cold email already works | Medium: sequence software often costs tens of dollars per user per month (check current price) | **High**: all email and call rules, at scale | 2–4 weeks |
| [LinkedIn (by hand)](linkedin.md) | Buyers with clear job titles who are active on LinkedIn | Free; paid search tools optional (check current price) | Medium: automation breaks the User Agreement | 1–3 weeks |
| [Reddit](reddit.md) | Hearing the problem in buyers' own words; reputation in niche groups | Free | Medium: self-promotion bans, no scraping | Months |
| [Communities (forums, Discord, Slack)](communities.md) | Practitioners who learn from peers: developers, ops, specialists | Free (some paid memberships) | Medium: each community's rules | 1–3 months |
| [News and funding](news-and-funding.md) | Timing: new money, new leaders, launches, regulations | Free (paid databases optional) | Low | Days |
| [Job posts](job-posts.md) | Timing and fit: companies hiring for the problem you solve | Free | Low (if you don't scrape job boards) | Days |
| [Events and webinars](events.md) | Industries with strong conferences, trade shows or user groups | Medium to high: tickets, travel, sponsorship; webinars low | Low to medium: never buy attendee lists | Weeks (around event dates) |
| [Partners and referrals](partners-and-referrals.md) | Users with happy customers, or buyers who trust a platform or adviser | Free to medium: referral fees or marketplace fees, if any | Low to medium: disclose paid referrals; some industries restrict fees | 1–3 months |
| [Content and inbound](inbound.md) | Buyers who research before buying, especially technical buyers | Free to low | Low: honest consent and claims | 2–6 months, then keeps working |

## How to choose (a quick guide for the assistant)

- **Need conversations this month?** Pair one research channel
  (`websites.md`, `job-posts.md` or `news-and-funding.md`) with one
  outreach channel (`cold-email.md`, `cold-calling.md` or `linkedin.md`).
- **Selling to developers or technical buyers?** Lean on `inbound.md`,
  `communities.md` and `partners-and-referrals.md`; use cold outreach
  sparingly and only with a strong signal.
- **Selling to local or small businesses?** `cold-calling.md`,
  `websites.md`, `events.md` (local meetups) and referrals.
- **Recipients mostly in Germany or Canada?** Avoid cold email; use
  events, inbound, partners, and (for Germany) calls only with a
  concrete reason to think the business wants the call.
- **User has happy customers already?** Start referrals this week; it's
  the cheapest, warmest channel.

*For example:* for a voice-AI API company such as Deepgram, a sensible
start might be `job-posts.md` (companies hiring speech engineers) plus
`inbound.md` (technical content developers search for) plus
`partners-and-referrals.md` (platforms that need transcription), with
cold email only where a fresh signal exists.
