# Scoring a lead (1 to 10)

Score on three things. Every point must rest on evidence you can link.

## A. Fit (0–4)
- 4: matches the best-customer pattern on industry, size, region and buyer
- 2: matches some of it
- 0: doesn't fit, or is a competitor, an existing customer or a partner to skip

## B. Timing signal (0–4)
- 4: a strong, recent, quoted signal (hiring for the exact role, a fresh
  funding round, a public complaint about the problem in the last month)
- 2: a medium or older signal
- 0: no signal, fit only

## C. Reachability (0–2)
- 2: a named business contact in the right role, found on a page you can
  cite
- 1: a general business inbox, contact form or main line
- 0: no lawful way to reach them yet

## Reading the total
**Fit 0 means stop.** A competitor, an existing customer, a partner to
skip, or a company outside the profile is excluded, whatever the other
scores add up to.

- **8–10:** contact this week.
- **5–7:** worth contacting, or watch for a stronger signal.
- **1–4:** don't save it unless the user asks. It's a guess.

## Each saved lead records
```
Company:
Website:
Score: A_/4 + B_/4 + C_/2 = _/10
Signal: "<exact quote>" (URL, date seen)
Fit notes: (labelled measured or inferred)
Contact: name, role, business email/phone, where found (URL)
Channel to use:
Status: new / drafted / approved / sent / replied / not interested / do-not-contact
```
